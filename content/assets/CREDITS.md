# Bilig 3D asset credits

Every binary asset in `content/assets/<topicKey>/` is listed here and in that
folder's `ASSET.json` (licence, author, source URL, download date,
modifications; TZ A-05). `content/build.mjs` refuses to hash an asset that has
no `ASSET.json` entry with a licence, an author and a source. Only clearly
free licences are accepted: CC0, public domain / US government works, and
CC BY (with the attribution below). No NC, ND, "editorial only" or
login-gated sources.

## human-heart

Files: `human-heart.glb`, `thumbs/human-heart.webp`, `thumbs/heart-section.webp`

> Heart and great vessels: "3D Reference Organ for Heart, Male v1.3"
> (<https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM564.WKNG.249>) and "3D Reference Organ for
> Blood Vasculature, Male v1.3" by Kristen Browne and Heidi Schlehlein,
> Human Reference Atlas / NIH HuBMAP (<https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library>),
> made from the Visible Human Male (National Library of Medicine). Licensed
> [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified by
> Bilig: subset of vessels, renamed parts, simplified, recoloured, section caps
> and schematic pericardium added.

- Downloaded 2026-10-15 from
  `https://cdn.humanatlas.io/digital-objects/ref-organ/heart-male/v1.3/assets/3d-vh-m-heart.glb`
  and
  `https://cdn.humanatlas.io/digital-objects/ref-organ/blood-vasculature-male/v1.3/assets/3d-vh-m-blood-vasculature.glb`.
- Modified: atlas colours with clearcoat, groove fat painted along the coronary
  vessels (vertex colours), flat caps where the section plane cuts the walls,
  per-part simplification.
- Pipeline: `scripts/assets/human-heart.mjs`.

## earth-layers

Files: `earth-layers.glb`, `thumbs/earth-layers.webp`

> Earth textures: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation
> (Reto Stöckli, NASA GSFC), December 2004, topography and bathymetry, and
> Blue Marble topography (elevation). Public domain (NASA imagery is not
> copyrighted).

- Layer geometry, cross-section texture, atmosphere halo, plate patches and
  earthquake markers: made by Bilig, released under CC0 1.0. Radii are to
  scale except the crust, which is exaggerated so that it is visible. Plates,
  mantle convection cells and epicentres are schematic.
- Downloaded 2026-10-15 from
  `https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73909/world.topo.bathy.200412.3x5400x2700.jpg`
  and 2026-10-17 from
  `https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73934/gebco_08_rev_elev_21600x10800.png`
  (turned into the crust's relief normal map).
- Pipeline: `scripts/assets/earth-layers.mjs`.

## animal-cell, plant-cell

Files: `animal-cell/models/animal-cell.glb`, `plant-cell/models/plant-cell.glb`

- Made by Bilig, released under CC0 1.0 (no attribution required): three.js
  geometry extending the procedural cells of the approved Bilig Workbench
  prototype, one named node per structure, colours from the concept hues.
  Organic shapes from seeded noise, per-vertex colour with baked shading and
  a procedural normal map, all generated by the script (no third-party
  textures).
- No free model with separate, named organelles was found: NIH 3D's
  "Animal Cell" is CC BY-NC-SA (non-commercial, excluded); other cell models
  are paid marketplace models or physical teaching models.
- Pipeline: `scripts/assets/cells.mjs` (`animal` / `plant`).

## glaciers

Files: `glaciers.glb`

- Made by Bilig, released under CC0 1.0. No third-party material.
- A schematic Tien Shan valley-glacier diorama generated by the pipeline:
  cirque and tributary glacier, accumulation zone (snow) and ablation zone
  (bare ice) split at the equilibrium line, crevasses, meltwater streams,
  lateral, medial and terminal moraines, a proglacial lake, an older terminal
  moraine and a braided river; a long section through snow, firn, ice and
  basal debris.
- No free glacier model with these separate parts was found (NASA 3D
  Resources, Smithsonian 3D, Poly Haven, Kenney, Wikimedia Commons; the
  marketplace models are paid or royalty-free, not CC0 / CC BY).
- Its land/ice colour texture is baked by the same script from seeded noise
  (no photos or scans), so it is CC0 too.
- Relief from ridged and eroded fractal noise; per-vertex colour from altitude,
  slope and exposure (rock, scree, meadow, snow) with baked cavity shading.
- Pipeline: `scripts/assets/glaciers.mjs` (shared kit `scripts/assets/diorama.mjs`).

## water-cycle

Files: `water-cycle.glb`

- Made by Bilig, released under CC0 1.0. No third-party material.
- A schematic diorama of the Issyk-Kul basin generated by the pipeline:
  snowy ranges with two glaciers, meltwater rivers, the lake, clouds with
  rain and snow, the Sun, a sky backdrop (atmosphere), an aquifer layer in
  the ground block, and arrows for evaporation, transpiration, vapour
  transport, infiltration and groundwater flow.
- No free water-cycle model with these separate parts was found (same
  libraries as above).
- Its land/glacier colour texture and sky gradient are baked by the same
  script from seeded noise (no photos or scans), so they are CC0 too.
- Relief and colouring as in the glacier diorama; layered cumulus clouds.
- Pipeline: `scripts/assets/water-cycle.mjs` (shared kit `scripts/assets/diorama.mjs`).
